# AI-Usage-Impact-Analysis-in-Education


## 🚀 Project Overview
This project analyzes how AI tools like ChatGPT impact student learning and academic performance.  
We built a data-driven system to identify risky AI usage patterns and improve student outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
With the rapid adoption of AI in education, students are increasingly relying on these tools.  
However, there is no structured way to monitor usage or understand its impact on academic performance.

---

##  Solution
We developed a **Multi-Factor AI Risk Model** that evaluates student behavior and assigns a risk score (0–100).

###  Factors Used:
- Daily AI Usage Hours  
- Impact on Grades  
- Trust in AI Tools  
- Professor Permission  
- Internet Access  

---

##  Risk Classification
- 80+ → Critical 🚨  
- 60–80 → High ⚠️  
- 40–60 → Medium 🟡  
- 20–40 → Low ℹ️  
- <20 → Safe ✅  

---

##  Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard provides:
- Usage vs Academic Impact  
- Trust vs Performance  
- State-wise Heatmap  
- Risk Segmentation  

---

##  Key Insights
- Moderate AI usage improves performance  
- Overuse leads to dependency and poor outcomes  
- Guided usage is more effective than unrestricted usage  

---

##  Tech Stack
- SQL (Data Cleaning & Transformation)  
- Power BI (Visualization & Dashboard)  

---

##  Case Study
We applied our model on a student case:
- High AI usage (4.5 hrs/day)  
- Negative grade impact (-3)  

After intervention:
- Usage reduced to 2–3 hrs  
- Grade improved to +2  
- Risk level reduced from High to Low  

---

##  Conclusion
AI is not harmful — uncontrolled usage is.  
Our system helps institutions monitor, control, and optimize AI usage for better learning outcomes.
